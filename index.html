Class 3

AI as Your Learning Partner

Learning • studying • school & everyday life

Mission

AI can help you learn — not learn for you

AI can explain hard topics, create examples, help you study, organize ideas, and make practice questions. You still need to think, check, and understand.

Learning Challenge

Pick something you want explained

Choose a topic and ask AI to explain it at your grade level.

Study Builder

Turn a topic into a study plan

Ask AI for a study guide, examples, flashcards, or practice questions.

Think First

Use AI responsibly for school

Use AI for explanations, examples, practice, brainstorming, feedback, and study plans — not for copying work you do not understand.

Home Mission

Use AI as a tutor

Choose one topic. Ask AI to teach it, then explain it back in your own words.

Class 5

Solving Problems with AI

Identify problems • generate ideas • compare solutions

Problem Finder

Choose a problem worth solving

Pick a problem at school, at home, in your neighborhood, on a team, or in everyday life.

Understand It

What is really happening?

Who is affected? Why does it happen? What would a good solution look like?

AI Prompt

Ask for options — not just one answer

Ask AI for several realistic solutions with benefits, challenges, and what you would need to begin.

Compare

Pick the strongest idea

Compare the best ideas and choose the one that makes the most sense.

Human Check

Does the solution work in real life?

Decide what needs to be changed before trying it.

Home Mission
